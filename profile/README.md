# Noxen Digital

**Noxen Digital** is an Istanbul-based digital marketing and web development agency. We help businesses in Türkiye and the Arab world get found on Google and recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. We work in English, Arabic and Turkish.

🌐 [noxendigital.com](https://noxendigital.com) · 🇹🇷 [Türkçe](https://noxendigital.com/tr) · 🇸🇦 [العربية](https://noxendigital.com/ar) · ✉️ info@noxendigital.com

## What we do

- **SEO**: technical audits, on-page optimization, local, e-commerce and multilingual SEO (hreflang, right-to-left Arabic layouts)
- **GEO (generative engine optimization)**: making brands visible and cited in AI answers
- **Web development**: Laravel, WordPress and Shopify sites built fast and SEO-ready
- **Ads & analytics**: Google Ads and Meta Ads with GA4 conversion tracking

## Things we build

- 🛠️ **[Nox Tools](https://noxendigital.com/tools)**: 100+ free SEO and GEO tools (hreflang checker, schema generator, llms.txt validator, AI visibility checks and more), in three languages
- 🩺 **[Catalog Doctor](https://apps.shopify.com/catalog-doctor-public)**: a Shopify app that scores catalog health, fixes missing SEO titles, ALT text and GTINs, and tracks how often AI assistants mention a store
- 📚 **[Guides](https://noxendigital.com/blog)**: practical SEO, GEO and web development guides in English, Arabic and Turkish
